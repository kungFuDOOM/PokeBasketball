Poké Jam — free Pokémon basketball in your browser

Poké Jam is a fan-made Pokémon basketball game. Draft a team of Pokémon and play fast 5-on-5 arcade hoops with NBA Jam-style dunks and type matchups. It runs in your browser on desktop or phone, with nothing to install.

Play Poké Jam

Ranked

Play online against other players in Ranked and climb the ranks.

Franchise

Build a roster, draft rookies and play a full season in Franchise mode, with the option to sim or watch games.

Quick Play

Jump into a single game against the CPU or a friend without setting up a season.
