Poké Jam controls

Every control for Poké Jam, the free Pokémon basketball game in your browser: keyboard, touch screen and gamepad. For tips and modes, see how to play.

Keyboard

MoveW A S D or arrow keys
Shoot / JumpJ or Space. Hold, then release when the meter hits green. On defense it jumps to block
Pass / SwitchK. On defense it switches the Pokémon you control
StealL
TurboHold Shift (left or right)
Special moveI or E when the special bar is full
PauseP or Esc

Combine Shift with the shoot, pass and steal keys for a dunk, an alley-oop pass or a shove.

Touch screen

On phones and tablets, an on-screen stick on the left moves your Pokémon. The buttons on the right match a gamepad:

StickMove
XShoot (Jump on defense)
APass (Switch on defense)
BSteal
YSpecial move
RBTurbo

Gamepad

Any controller your browser recognizes works. Plug it in or pair it, then press a button. Button names below use the Xbox-style layout.

MoveLeft stick or D-pad
Shoot / JumpX
Pass / SwitchA
StealB
Special moveY
TurboEither bumper or either trigger
PauseStart
