Weekly challenges and the card drawing

No purchase necessary. Last updated: November 3, 2026.

No purchase is needed. You do not have to buy a Rank Shield, burn PJAM, or hold PJAM to enter. Holding PJAM is one way to earn extra entries. Playing is the other way, and it can earn the same extra entries.

When a week starts

A week runs from Monday 00:00 Pacific time to the next Monday 00:00 Pacific time. The challenges screen shows the time this week ends.

One base entry

Finish every weekly challenge to get 1 entry. The challenges screen shows the numbers in effect. The defaults are:

  • Play 3 offline games (Quick Play or Franchise, played by you. Sim and watch do not count).
  • Finish 3 online games.
  • Do 1 social action: share a result, or finish a social match.
  • Climb 1 rank on the Ranked ladder (one step), or already be at Zenith, the top rank.

Guests are not entered. Sign in with the account you play on.

Bonus entries

Up to 5 bonus entries a week, so 6 entries in all. The two ways add together, then stop at 5:

  • Holding PJAM. 1 bonus entry per 1,000,000 PJAM in your linked wallet at the moment the winner is drawn. The mint is GQe9QiBBV4G6bRUCxx2JgKrzT7G7RD17FJquBWgVpump. The balance is read on the server from Solana. The game does not take the tokens. Whole tokens count; a fraction of a token does not.
  • Playing. The same bonus entries, with no tokens. Each one takes 10 extra online games past the weekly online challenge, and 3 online wins that week. Both are required for each entry. A regular player can do this. It takes real play.

Someone who holds enough PJAM does not have to grind. Someone who holds none can still earn every bonus entry by playing. The operator can change these amounts; the challenges screen shows the live ones.

Odds

Your chance is your number of entries divided by everyone's entries that week. A player with 4 entries is four times as likely as a player with 1. If nobody finishes the challenges, there is no drawing.

The drawing and the prize

After the week ends, a moderator runs the drawing. The server makes a random seed, lines the players up by account id, and walks a ticket through the entries. The seed and the entry list are saved so the pick can be checked. The moderator sees the entrants and the winner.

The prize is a Pokémon card. The operator sends it by hand. The game does not collect a shipping address, a phone number, or a real name, and it does not pay or ship anything automatically.
