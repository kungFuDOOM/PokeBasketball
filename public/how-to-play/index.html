How to play Poké Jam

Poké Jam is a free Pokémon basketball game that runs in your browser. Pick a team of Pokémon and play 5-on-5 arcade basketball. Here are the controls.

Keyboard

MoveWASD or arrow keys
Shoot / BlockJ or Space. Hold, then release when the meter hits green
DunkHold Shift and shoot near the rim
Pass / SwitchK. Add Shift to throw an alley-oop to a teammate near the rim
StealL. Add Shift to shove (type matchups apply)
TurboShift
Special moveI or E when the special bar is full
PauseP or Esc

Gamepad and phone

Gamepad: X shoots, A passes, B steals, Y is your special, and any bumper or trigger is turbo. On phones, use the on-screen stick and buttons.

Modes

  • Quick Play: a single game, no season.
  • Franchise: draft a roster and play a full season.
  • Ranked: online games against other players. More on Ranked.
