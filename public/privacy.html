Poké Jam privacy policy and terms

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version: Poké Jam is a free browser game with no accounts, no payments, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. Your saved games stay on your own device. Nobody, including us, can see them.

Privacy policy

What the game stores

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage on your own device so you can pick up where you left off:

WhatWhy
Team name, difficulty and quarter lengthRemembers your settings
Franchise save (rosters, schedule, standings, stats, levels)Lets you continue your season
Draft in progressLets you leave and resume a draft
Sound on or offRemembers your audio choice

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. It is not used to identify or track you.

Deleting your data

Press Delete saved data at the bottom of the title screen to erase everything the game has stored. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings does the same. There is no account to close because there are no accounts.

Third-party services

None while you play. The game's code, fonts, sprites and sounds are all served by this website itself. The game does not load analytics, advertising, social media widgets, payment processors or session recording tools.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard technical information when your browser loads the site, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in server logs for security. See the GitHub Pages data collection notice and GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies

Poké Jam does not set cookies. The local storage described above is used only for features you choose to use, such as saving your franchise.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the game ever adds features that collect data, such as online accounts, this page will list every service involved before those features go live.
