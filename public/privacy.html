Poké Jam privacy policy and terms

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version: Poké Jam is a free browser game. Its sponsor and ad spots are text only, and there is no ad network or ad tracking. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics, which gives us anonymous traffic stats and sets cookies. Playing on your own (Quick Play, Franchise, drafts) needs no account, and those saves stay on your own device. If you choose to play online, the game makes you an anonymous account and keeps what online play needs, listed below. The only other things the game sends are a short crash report if it breaks and anonymous gameplay counts.

Privacy policy

What the game stores

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage on your own device so you can pick up where you left off:

WhatWhy
Team name, difficulty and quarter lengthRemembers your settings
Franchise save (rosters, schedule, standings, stats, levels)Lets you continue your season
Draft in progressLets you leave and resume a draft
Sound, music and announcer on or offRemembers your audio choices
Online sign-in (only if you play online)Keeps your anonymous online account on this device

Your saves and settings never leave your device. They are not used to identify or track you.

Online play (only if you choose it)

Online play is optional. When you first open it, the game makes an anonymous account for you (no email, no password, no real name). Then it keeps:

WhatWhyHow long
A random account ID, and the trainer name you pickSo you can be matched. Your opponent sees your trainer name. Names are checked for slurs and impersonation, and one that isn't allowed becomes "Trainer".While the account exists
Your matches: who played, when, the resultTo run matches, record results, and settle disputesWhile the account exists
Reports you make or receive, players you block, and any suspensionTo stop cheating and harassment. Reports are read only by the game's moderators, and the player you report isn't told who reported them.While the account exists
Short-lived counters, and a pass from the bot checkTo stop spam and bots (for example, how many rooms you made in the last 10 minutes)Minutes to a day
Lobby and match data: room codes, the teams each player picks, scores and resultsTo run the match, show the result to both players, and settle disputesWhile the account exists

During a match, your game and your opponent's exchange controller inputs, either directly (WebRTC), through a relay (TURN) server, or through Supabase, so the two games stay in step. A direct connection shows your IP address to your opponent's device; a relay keeps it hidden. Nothing you type is sent to the other player except your trainer name.

Your IP address and browser details are also processed by Supabase, Cloudflare (bot check) and the relay provider whenever online play connects to them, as described under third-party services. We do not use them to identify you.

Crash reports

If the game crashes (even if you never use online play), it sends a short crash report to our Supabase project: the error message and where in the code it happened, the page, which version of the game, and your browser's user agent (its name and version). No account, name or game data is included. We use reports only to fix bugs, and delete them after 30 days.

Gameplay counts

When you play on your own, the game sends anonymous gameplay counts to our Supabase project: that a game started or finished, the mode (Quick Play, Franchise, Season Draft), whether it was played, watched or simmed, and franchise season results (how far your team got). Each count is tagged with a random device id stored in your browser. No name, email, IP address or other personal info is stored with it. Clearing site data (or Delete saved data) resets the id. We use the counts only to see how much the game is played.

Deleting your data

Press Delete saved data at the bottom of the title screen to erase everything the game has stored on your device, including the online sign-in. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings does the same. To have an online account's records deleted from our servers, email us at the address below and say which trainer name it was (use the same device if you can, so we can match the account). We delete the account and its match records within 30 days of confirming the request. Crash reports hold no account information and are deleted after 30 days anyway.

Third-party services

Playing on your own uses none, apart from the crash reports and gameplay counts above, Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics. This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track people across sites, to count visits. The game's code, fonts, sprites and sounds are all served by this website itself. The game does not load ad networks, social media widgets or session recording tools. Payments, if any, go straight on-chain (see below).

Online play uses these services, which receive your IP address and browser information as part of providing them:

  • Supabase runs the online accounts, the database and the match messages (Supabase privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile is a bot check that may run before you sign in or start a match. Cloudflare checks your browser and IP address to tell people from bots (Turnstile privacy addendum).
  • Cloudflare TURN relay passes match traffic between you and your opponent, so neither of you sees the other's IP address. It doesn't keep the game data (Cloudflare privacy policy).

Ads and sponsors

Sponsor and ad spots (the courtside LED board and the menu ticker) are sold by kungFuDOOM. Ads are text only. No ad network or ad tracking is used, and sponsors don't get player data.

Courtside board spots can be booked on the Advertise page and paid either by burning $PJAM tokens or in USDC, on Solana, from your own wallet. Other sponsor spots are arranged directly and may be paid in crypto (SOL). Bookings and payments are final, with no refunds unless the law requires one.

When you book a spot, we keep your wallet address, your ad text and colour, the dates booked, the plan and payment method, the price and the on-chain transaction signature. We use them to show your ad, prove it was paid, and handle reports. Booked ads, with their wallet address, are public on the Advertise page while they run, and the payment itself is public on the blockchain. To limit spam, the Advertise page's requests (including reports) are counted against a salted hash of your IP address, never the IP itself; the counters last minutes to a day. Reports are read only by moderators. Booking records are kept as payment records; email us to ask about them.

Payments

The game is free to play. Any purchases are optional. If optional paid items are offered, crypto payments go on-chain from your own wallet. We never hold your keys. Blockchain transactions are public and usually can't be reversed.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 for anonymous traffic stats: which pages are viewed, rough location (country or city, worked out from your IP address) and device type. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off, and the site shows no Google ads or AdSense. Google Analytics does not run when the site is embedded on another website. Google processes this data under the Google privacy policy. To opt out on every site, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard technical information when your browser loads the site, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in server logs for security. See the GitHub Pages data collection notice and GitHub's privacy statement.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) on this site to count visits and tell new visitors from returning ones. The game itself sets no other cookies. The local storage described above is used only for features you choose to use, such as saving your franchise.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Playing on your own collects no personal information beyond the anonymous traffic stats above. Online play uses an anonymous account, a trainer name and the match records above, and asks for no email, real name or contact details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Before any new feature collects data, this page will list what it collects and every service involved.
