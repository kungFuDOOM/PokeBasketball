Coming soon

Not shipped yet. Nothing in this section is live.

  • New regions. A new region on November 23, then a new generation every 2 weeks. No region after Hoenn is in the game yet.
  • Pack Battles. Still marked coming soon in the game. The plan is to open a pack, pick your squad, and the winner takes the cards.

November 11, 2026

  • Menu ticker. Paid ads that are live now lead the menu's bottom ticker, ahead of the $PJAM line. A paid ad with a link can be tapped.

November 10, 2026

  • 3-Point Contest link. The contest also opens straight from pokejam.xyz/#3pt.

November 9, 2026

  • Johto and Hoenn. Franchise adds Johto and Hoenn, eight gyms in each. The Johto finale is Champion Lance, and the Hoenn finale is Champion Steven. That is 16 new badges. Win all eight gym badges and the title in the same season to unlock the next region: Johto, then Hoenn. Win the Hoenn championship and the next season returns to Kanto. Your team, levels and unlocks stay, and the badges reset.
  • Kabuto King. A limited featured character in Quick Play, the 3-Point Contest and Franchise. Available from November 9 until November 23, Pacific time.
  • Weekly prize. Open Profile, then Challenges. That screen shows this week's prize, a Collector Crypt card.

November 6, 2026

  • 3-Point Contest. One shooter, five racks, sixty seconds. Open it from the menu.

November 3, 2026

  • Weekly challenges. Sign in with your wallet, not as a guest, then open Profile and Challenges. Finish the week's challenges for an entry in the free Collector Crypt card drawing.

November 2, 2026

  • Ranked. Ranked is live. Five placement matches, then a climb from Bronze to Zenith. The first time you leave a Ranked game early, you get a warning. After that, Ranked matchmaking pauses for 15 minutes, then 1 day, 7 days, and 14 days. One early leave drops off for every 14 days you go without another. A pause already running is not cut short.

October 31, 2026

  • Training and the tutorial. Training sits on the Quick Play card: seven drills, up to 3 stars each, or Run all. The first time you open it, you go straight into the tutorial. After that, the "New? Try the tutorial" hint hides.

October 29, 2026

  • Team names. A name that isn't allowed shows "That team name isn't allowed. Please pick another." You stay on that screen until you pick a different one. A blocked name already in a save, a share link, or an opponent's name shows as the default team name instead. Scoreboard abbreviations are checked the same way.
  • Pre-game intro. A short screen shows both teams' lineups before tip-off. It plays before every Franchise game. Skip ends it right away. In an online game it ends early only when both players press Skip.

October 28, 2026

  • Watch. Franchise and Quick Play now have Watch next to Play and Sim. Both teams are controlled by the CPU. The speed button cycles 1x, 2x and 4x, and Skip jumps to the result. The result is tagged Watched.
  • Share. The final results screen has a Share button. On a phone it opens the share sheet. Otherwise you can post the score on X or copy a link. The text includes the score and the player of the game when there is one.
  • Small screens. The scoreboard keeps both scores visible on a small screen, including landscape, and stays in the top strip.

October 27, 2026

  • Online games. The game waits a beat based on the connection, sends controls more often, and shows a ping meter during the game. If online is busy, it says so and solo games still work. A short bot check can appear before the first sign-in or before matchmaking.
  • Courtside and the menu. The courtside banner is split into a $PJAM half and a YOUR AD HERE half, and the text no longer clips. The menu ticker leads with $PJAM. The footer shows the contract address, with Copy.
  • Advertise. The contact page lists ad prices and a SOL address you can copy.

October 26, 2026

  • Launch. PokeJam is live! Franchise and Quick Play are open to everyone, free in your browser on phone or desktop.
  • Contact. A Contact page is linked from the menu, with email and a way to DM on X.
  • GitHub. The menu footer links to the game's repository.
  • Ticker. The menu ticker has a YOUR AD HERE sponsor slot.
