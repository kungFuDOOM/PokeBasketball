Rank Shield purchases (terms of sale)

These terms apply when you buy a Rank Shield. You see them before you pay.

  • What you get. A Rank Shield is a digital item in the game. A pack adds the number of shields shown at checkout to your account. Each shield can save the points of one lost ranked match, under the rules shown in the game.
  • One use per 24 hours. At most one Rank Shield can be used in any rolling 24 hours, whether you bought it or earned it. You can hold at most 14, and buy at most 42 per ranked season.
  • No cash value. Rank Shields are not money, can't be exchanged for money, and are not real property. You can't sell, trade, gift or transfer them.
  • Tied to your account. Shields belong to the signed-in account that bought them. We can't move them to another account. If an account is closed for breaking the rules, its shields are lost.
  • Things can change. We may change how Rank Shields work, rebalance or remove them, or end the game. If we remove purchased shields that you haven't used, we will refund them.
  • Who may buy. You must be 18 or older, or have a parent's or guardian's permission, and you confirm this before checkout. We don't target children under 13 and don't knowingly sell to them.
  • Payment. Payments are taken in US dollars on Stripe's secure checkout page. We never see your card. Prices are shown before you pay, and any taxes due are included or shown there.
  • Refunds and chargebacks. See Refunds. A refund or a chargeback removes the shields it paid for, and a dispute can stop you from buying again.

Refunds

Rank Shields are a digital item. Even so:

  • Unused shields bought in the last 14 days can be refunded on request. Email kungfudoom@mail.grokbot.com with the email on your Stripe receipt and your trainer name.
  • Used shields can't be refunded. If you used some of a pack, the unused rest can be refunded in proportion to the price of the pack.
  • When a payment is refunded or disputed, the shields it paid for are removed from your account. Shields already used can't be taken back, and we don't undo the rank points a used shield saved.
  • If a payment succeeded but your shields didn't arrive, or you were charged twice, email us and we will fix it or refund you.
  • Please contact us before you ask your bank to reverse a charge. A dispute stops purchases on your account until it's sorted out.
