Poké Jam FAQ

Quick answers about Poké Jam, the free Pokémon basketball game in your browser.

What is Poké Jam?

Poké Jam is a free, fan-made Pokémon basketball game. You draft a team of Pokémon and play 5-on-5 arcade basketball with big dunks, turbo and special moves.

Is Poké Jam free?

Yes. Poké Jam is free to play.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Poké Jam runs in your web browser on desktop and mobile, with no download or install.

What are the controls?

On a keyboard, move with WASD or the arrow keys, shoot with J or Space, pass with K, steal with L, turbo with Shift and use your special move with I or E. Touch and gamepad controls are also supported. See the controls page for the full list.

What game modes are there?

Quick Play is a single game against a Gym Leader or the Elite Four. Franchise lets you draft a roster and play a full season. Ranked is online play against other players.

What is Ranked?

Ranked is the online competitive mode. You play live head-to-head games against other players. Your first 5 games are placement games, and after that you climb through tiers and divisions. More on Ranked.

What are the Ranked leagues?

Ranked tiers go Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond and Pokémon Master. Every tier except Pokémon Master has three divisions.

What is the official site?

The official site is pokejam.xyz. Poké Jam is a fan-made game and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company.
