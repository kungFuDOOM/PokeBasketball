Ranked Pokémon basketball

Ranked is the online competitive mode in Poké Jam, a free fan-made Pokémon basketball game that runs in your browser. You play live head-to-head games against other players and climb the ranks.

What to expect

  • Live online games against another player, with the same 5-on-5 arcade rules as the rest of the game.
  • Leaving a Ranked game early is discouraged: you get a warning first, then matchmaking pauses grow longer if it keeps happening.
  • If online is busy, solo modes (Quick Play and Franchise) still work.
