# Johto and Hoenn starter buffs

Kanto starters are unchanged. Venusaur and Blastoise are 88. Charizard is 91. These six lines move toward that band. No final passes Charizard, and none reaches the 97 legendaries.

Overall is `overall()` in `src/data/pokemon.ts`. Stats are speed, shot, inside, pass, defense, block, rebound, handle. Every change is a buff or a hold.

## Chikorita line

Grass bulk. Meganium's top stat is rebound. He was a Hustle Big only because rebound was 88. Rebound is now 92, so he is a Glass Cleaner on purpose. Block stays 88 and pass stays 81, so he does not become a Rim Protector or a Point Center. Venusaur remains the higher grass final at 88.
